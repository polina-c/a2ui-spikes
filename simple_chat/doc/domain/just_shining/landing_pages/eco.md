# Just Shining Eco

A 60 cm dishwasher that uses 6.8 liters and 0.62 kWh per eco cycle.

## Who it is for

The Eco uses about a third less water and a third less electricity per cycle
than the Classic. It weighs the load and measures how dirty the water is,
then sets the water and the time to match, and it dries with a heat exchanger
rather than a heating element.

Pick it for a household on a water meter, on a well, or in a place where water
is restricted in summer. It is also the one to pick if you simply want to use
less water and less power.

The savings are real but slow. At four cycles a week the Eco uses about 400
liters and 90 kWh less per year than the Classic, which at normal utility
rates is a small amount of money. The difference in price takes years to come
back.

## Specifications

* Size: 60 cm wide, 60 cm deep, 85 cm high
* Capacity: 14 place settings
* Noise: 42 dB
* Water: 6.8 liters per eco cycle
* Energy: 0.62 kWh per eco cycle
* Energy class: A
* Drying: heat exchanger, no heating element
* Sensors: load weight and water turbidity, adjusting water volume and cycle length
* Programs: eco (3 h 40 min), auto, quick 60, intensive, glass, hygiene, half load, rinse, self-clean

## What is in the box

The machine, a 1.5 m inlet hose, a 1.8 m drain hose, a cutlery basket, and the
panel screws for under-counter fitting.
