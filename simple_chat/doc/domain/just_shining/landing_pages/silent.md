# Just Shining Silent

A 60 cm dishwasher that runs at 39 dB, the quietest machine we sell.

## Who it is for

The Silent is for kitchens that are open to a room where people sleep, work,
or watch television, and for anyone who starts a cycle at night. At 39 dB it
is about as loud as a fridge, and the difference from the 46 dB Classic is
large: every 3 dB is roughly a doubling of sound energy.

It ends the cycle without a beep. A light on the floor in front of the machine
goes out instead, so a cycle that finishes at two in the morning wakes nobody.

## Specifications

* Size: 60 cm wide, 60 cm deep, 85 cm high
* Capacity: 14 place settings
* Noise: 39 dB
* Water: 9.5 liters per eco cycle
* Energy class: A
* Motor: brushless inverter
* Programs: eco (3 h 20 min), night (4 h), auto, quick 60, intensive, glass, hygiene, rinse, self-clean
* Delay start: 3, 6, or 9 hours
* Insulation: double-walled tub with a bitumen damping layer

## What is in the box

The machine, a 1.5 m inlet hose, a 1.8 m drain hose, a cutlery basket, and the
panel screws for under-counter fitting.

## Delivery and warranty

Free delivery in five working days. Two-year warranty on parts and labor, and
five years on the motor.

We do not install. Any plumber can connect the machine to a cold water line
and a drain in about an hour.
