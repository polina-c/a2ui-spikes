# Just Shining Classic

The standard 60 cm dishwasher, and the one most households end up with.

## Who it is for

The Classic fits the 60 cm gap that kitchens are usually built around and
takes 14 place settings, which covers a household of three or four running it
once a day. Pick it when nothing about the kitchen or the household is
unusual: it is the cheapest of the full-size models and it does the same job
as the others.

The quick 60 program washes and dries a normal load in an hour, which is the
program most owners use on weekdays.

## Specifications

* Size: 60 cm wide, 60 cm deep, 85 cm high
* Capacity: 14 place settings
* Noise: 46 dB
* Water: 10.5 liters per eco cycle
* Energy class: C
* Programs: eco (3 h 10 min), auto, quick 60, intensive, glass, hygiene, rinse, self-clean
* Baskets: lower basket with folding tines, upper basket adjustable over two heights

## What is in the box

The machine, a 1.5 m inlet hose, a 1.8 m drain hose, a cutlery basket, and the
panel screws for under-counter fitting.

## Delivery and warranty

Free delivery in five working days. Two-year warranty on parts and labor.

We do not install. Any plumber can connect the machine to a cold water line
and a drain in about an hour.
