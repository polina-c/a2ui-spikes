# Just Shining Family

A 60 cm dishwasher with a taller tub and a third rack, for 16 place settings.

## Who it is for

The Family takes the same 60 cm gap as the Classic but holds 16 place
settings instead of 14, and adds a shallow third rack at the top for cutlery
and small utensils. That rack frees the space where a cutlery basket would
otherwise stand, so pots and bowls fit in the lower basket.

Pick it for a household of five or more, or for anyone who cooks enough to run
a normal dishwasher twice in a day. The extra capacity is worth more than the
price difference when it saves a second cycle.

## Specifications

* Size: 60 cm wide, 60 cm deep, 85 cm high
* Capacity: 16 place settings
* Noise: 44 dB
* Water: 11.5 liters per eco cycle
* Energy class: B
* Programs: eco (3 h 30 min), auto, quick 60, intensive, pots and pans, glass, hygiene, half load, rinse, self-clean
* Baskets: third cutlery rack, height-adjustable middle basket, folding tines in both baskets

## What is in the box

The machine, a 1.5 m inlet hose, a 1.8 m drain hose, a bottle-wash attachment
for the upper basket, and the panel screws for under-counter fitting.

## Delivery and warranty

Free delivery in five working days. Two-year warranty on parts and labor.

We do not install. Any plumber can connect the machine to a cold water line
and a drain in about an hour.
