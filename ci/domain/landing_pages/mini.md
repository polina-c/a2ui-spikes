# Just Shining Mini

A countertop dishwasher for kitchens where nothing can be plumbed in.

## Who it is for

The Mini sits on the counter and connects to the kitchen faucet with the
adapter in the box. Nothing is fitted under the cabinets and nothing is
drilled, so it works in a rented apartment, a studio kitchen, or an office
kitchenette. It holds 6 place settings, about one day of dishes for two people.

If the faucet does not take the adapter, the Mini has a 5 liter tank that you
fill by hand.

## Specifications

* Size: 55 cm wide, 50 cm deep, 44 cm high
* Capacity: 6 place settings
* Noise: 49 dB
* Water: 6.5 liters per eco cycle
* Energy class: F
* Programs: eco (1 h 50 min), quick 40, intensive, glass, rinse, self-clean
* Connection: faucet adapter, or the built-in 5 liter tank

## What is in the box

The machine, a faucet adapter with a 1.5 m inlet hose, a drain hose, and a
cutlery basket.

## Delivery and warranty

Free delivery in five working days. Two-year warranty on parts and labor.
No installation is needed.
