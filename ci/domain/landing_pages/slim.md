# Just Shining Slim

A 45 cm dishwasher for a narrow gap between cabinets.

## Who it is for

The Slim is the model for kitchens that have a water connection but only
45 cm of width, which is common in older apartments and in galley kitchens.
It takes 10 place settings, enough for a household of two or three running it
once a day.

It can stand on its own or slide under a counter once the top panel is
removed.

## Specifications

* Size: 45 cm wide, 60 cm deep, 85 cm high
* Capacity: 10 place settings
* Noise: 44 dB
* Water: 9 liters per eco cycle
* Energy class: C
* Programs: eco (3 h), auto, intensive, quick 40, glass, hygiene, rinse, self-clean
* Baskets: lower basket with folding tines, upper basket adjustable over two heights

## What is in the box

The machine, a 1.5 m inlet hose, a 1.8 m drain hose, a cutlery basket, and the
panel screws for under-counter fitting.

## Delivery and warranty

Free delivery in five working days. Two-year warranty on parts and labor.

We do not install. Any plumber can connect the machine to a cold water line
and a drain in about an hour.
